Why Is Healthy Food Important?

Eating a balanced diet is important for good health. Our body needs different nutrients such as carbohydrates, proteins, vitamins, minerals, fats, and water.

Healthy Foods to Include

Fruits and Vegetables

Fruits and vegetables are rich in vitamins, minerals, fibre, and antioxidants. Try to include different colourful fruits and vegetables in your meals.

Whole Grains

Foods such as oats, brown rice, and whole-wheat bread provide carbohydrates and fibre that help give us energy.

Protein-Rich Foods

Eggs, beans, lentils, fish, dairy products, nuts, and seeds provide protein, which helps our body grow and repair itself.

Water

Water is essential for our body. Drinking enough water helps us stay hydrated and supports many important body functions.

Nutrition Tip

Try to eat a variety of foods instead of eating the same foods every day. A colourful and balanced plate can provide many of the nutrients your body needs.

Healthy Eating Habits

  • Eat a variety of foods.
  • Include fruits and vegetables in your meals.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Do not skip meals regularly.
  • Choose whole-grain foods when possible.
  • • Enjoy treats in moderation.
